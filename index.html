Lucas Schorling

PhD student · University of Oxford

I am a PhD student in Engineering Science at the University of Oxford, working on AI for physics with the Machine Learning Research Group and the Quantum Device Lab. My research sits at the intersection of machine learning, mathematics, physics and quantum computing, from learning the dynamics of quantum systems to physics-inspired optimizers for deep learning. I spent six months as a visiting guest researcher at the Max Planck Institute for the Science of Light, working on an AI scientist for quantum systems.

If you would like to chat, feel free to reach out on LinkedIn.
